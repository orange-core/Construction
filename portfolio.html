Portfolio intelligence

Patterns we have learned from the work.

01

Live environments need sequencing.

Civil and commercial projects rarely happen in a vacuum. Access, occupants, utilities and neighbouring work fronts shape the programme.

02

Long-lead decisions need visibility.

Procurement risk is easier to manage when package status is connected to the programme rather than kept in a separate spreadsheet.

03

Quality is progressive.

The cleanest handovers are created by closing evidence and defects throughout the build, not in the final week.
